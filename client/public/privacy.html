Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Who we are

To-Day is a personal task management application. If you have questions about this policy, contact us at support@embitted.com.

2. Data we collect

  • Account data: your email address, used to create and authenticate your account.
  • Task data: the text of tasks and lists you create, including completion and importance flags.
  • Usage data: anonymous page-view and interaction statistics collected by Umami Analytics (no cookies, no personal identifiers).

3. How we use your data

  • To provide and maintain the service (authentication, storing your tasks).
  • To improve the application using anonymised aggregate analytics.
  • We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Data storage

Your data is stored in Supabase (PostgreSQL), hosted within the European Union. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest.

5. Data retention

Your data is retained as long as your account exists. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us.

6. Your rights

Under GDPR you have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. Contact us at support@embitted.com to exercise these rights.

7. Third-party services

  • Supabase — database & authentication (privacy policy)
  • Umami Analytics — privacy-friendly, cookie-free analytics (privacy policy)

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects the latest revision. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
